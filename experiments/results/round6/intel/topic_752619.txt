Has anyone else noticed that the simulators appear to model pressure-dependent VAV HVAC systems?

In other words, these seem to represent older HVAC designs, perhaps dating back to the 1980s, when VAV boxes often operated without airflow measurement and air volume control? … IE., its just air damper control direct for a Zone PID.

From what I can tell, the simulator has no airflow measurements, airflow setpoints, or VAV discharge air temperature sensors.

As a result, implementing a realistic True version of ASHRAE Guideline 36 trim-and-respond control sequences doesn't seem feasible, at least not in the way these strategies would operate in a modern pressure-independent VAV system.

Essentially, we're working with a legacy-style HVAC system simulator that doesn't appear to reflect modern ASHRAE 90.1 energy efficiency requirements or ASHRAE 62.1 ventilation requirements.

Is anyone else interpreting the simulator this way, or am I missing something in the available observations and control inputs?
